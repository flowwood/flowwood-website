Shop concepts, round two

Six premium directions. Each shows the top of the shop, the first collection and how the products are presented. The way you shop is the same in all of them: filter by collection, sort, choose a size and finish, add to cart. Only the look and feel changes.

01

Gallery

Warm bone background, everything centred with lots of air, like a gallery or a design museum. Product names in spaced capitals, prices quiet, the filter as understated text.
